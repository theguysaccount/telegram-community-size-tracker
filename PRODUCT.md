# Product

**User.** Someone deciding whether to trust, join or invest around a Telegram community: a crypto investor checking a project's "community size", a journalist, a project team comparing itself with peers, an agency running a campaign.

**Painful job.** "Is this community as big as it says, and is its growth real?" Telegram shows only today's number. Bought members and giveaway spikes look like organic growth unless you can see the history.

**Promise.** For each tracked channel: today's public count, a chart going back up to two years, growth over 1 day to 1 year, and a flag when a change is far outside the channel's normal pace, with a plain note that a flag can't say why.

**Wedge.** A search-indexed page per channel ("hamster kombat telegram subscribers", "notcoin channel members history"), embeddable badges projects can show, and open CSVs others can cite.

**Why now.** Telegram mini-app projects (Notcoin, Hamster Kombat, DOGS, Blum) built tens of millions of subscribers in months and many have since shrunk; the Telegram News channel itself fell from 13.1M (Nov 2024) to 9.1M (Nov 2026) in this data. Size claims are everywhere, history is not.

**Activation metric.** An embed code copied or a channel page shared (instrumented; counted once the GoatCounter key is set).

**First revenue event.** None on Pages. The slot is a support link. The paid version (hourly sampling, watchlists, alerts, exports) needs other hosting and Stripe (OPERATIONS.md, key 2).

**What v1 won't do.** Track people or personal channels, read messages or member lists, sample hourly (GitHub's scheduler can't promise it on this account), or say whether a spike was bought.
