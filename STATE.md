# State (2026-11-22)

- **What existed before:** a five-word note from January 2022 ("Telegram community size graph tracker"). No code, no domain.
- **Reused:** the site toolkit from the AI Privacy Scorecard and Platform Defaults (built the same day): browser checks, keep-alive, IndexNow, share cards, workflow shapes.
- **Works:** 29 public channels and groups sampled twice a day from t.me; up to 24 months of monthly history from Internet Archive captures; per-channel pages with charts, growth, unusual-change flags, CSVs and badges; RSS; open data; weekly self-test that restarts sampling when data is stale.
- **Known gaps:** flags need a few days of twice-daily samples per channel before they can fire; sampling is twice daily, not hourly; the paid alerting version needs other hosting.
