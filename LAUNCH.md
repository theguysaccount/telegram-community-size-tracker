# Launch

Machine-driven mode: nothing below is posted, sent or bought by this project.

## Live

- https://theguysaccount.github.io/telegram-community-size-tracker/ · feed `/feed.xml` · open data `/data/channels.json` · CSVs `/data/history/<handle>.csv` · badges `/embed/<handle>.svg`

## Custom domain

Owned by the domain rollout session: one repository variable (`SITE_URL`) plus the Pages domain. Exact steps in OPERATIONS.md, key 1.

## Machine distribution running

- A page per channel titled "<Title> (@handle) Telegram subscribers: size and growth", with Dataset and Breadcrumb structured data, canonical URLs and per-channel share cards showing the count.
- Embeddable badges projects can put on their own sites; open CSVs others can cite.
- IndexNow after every deploy (twice a day, with fresh counts); the account's host-root robots.txt lists the sitemap.
- RSS of unusual changes and newly tracked channels.

## First 1,000 visitors (machine-only)

Per-channel pages rank for project-name searches; each new suggestion adds a page; badges on project sites link back. No posts, outreach or ads.
